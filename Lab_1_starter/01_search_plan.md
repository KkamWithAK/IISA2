# Search plan

Problem sentence:  People trying to communicate emotion in social VR struggle to express subtle non-verbal cues because current avatars lack real-time facial and body tracking.

## Concept blocks (two or three terms each; take synonyms from the first abstracts you read)

| Block | What it covers | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| A | the person's behaviour | The user wants communicates with another player|Non-verbal communication, emotional expresion, facial expression|
| B | the system's property | The system mimics the user's movement in real time, but fails to porttay fine movement and facial expressions|mimic, body tracking, real-time tracking,  avatar motion|
| C | the setting | The interaction takes place in a VR program with multiple users| Social VR, Virtual Reality, VR|

## One natural-language question (for tools that read questions)
How does the accuracy of real-time facial and body tracking affect the communication and non-verbal cues between users in social VR environments?

## One Boolean query (for tools that match strings: OR inside a block, AND between blocks)
("non-verbal communication" OR "emotional expression" OR "facial expression") AND (mimic OR "body tracking" OR "real-time tracking" OR "avatar motion") AND ("Social VR" OR "Virtual Reality" OR VR)
A block with no synonyms is probably your project's name, not a concept.
