## Screening criteria (written before screening)
1.  Domain: The paper must focus on what hapens within VR
2.  Behaviour: The paper must focus on non-verbal comunication (body language/facial expressions)
3.  Measure: the paper must measure the effect of non-verbal tracking fidelity agaist social outcomes
